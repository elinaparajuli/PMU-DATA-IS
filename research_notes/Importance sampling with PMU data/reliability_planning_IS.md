# Importance Sampling and Rare-Event Monte Carlo for Power System Reliability and Transmission Planning Risk (focus: IEEE Transactions, 2020-2026)

Method note: IEEE Xplore and arXiv could not be reached directly from the research environment (DNS failures). Verification therefore relies on search-engine snippets, institutional repositories (OSTI, NSF-PAR, CWI, PUC-Rio, XJTU, NTU), and bibliographic mirrors. Items marked **[unverified]** are recalled bibliographic details that could not be confirmed against a primary record in this session. Speed-up figures are reported only where a source stated them.

## Q1. Foundational IEEE Trans. papers on CE-IS / IS / subset simulation / splitting in reliability, and reported speed-ups

### Takeaway
The CE-IS line for composite generation-transmission (G&T) reliability starts with Leite da Silva's group at PUC-Rio/UNIFEI (TPWRS 2013, generating-capacity and composite versions). Subset simulation (Hua, Bie, Au et al., TPWRS 2015) and splitting for cascading blackouts (Kim, Bucklew, Dobson, TPWRS 2013; Shortle et al., TPWRS 2014) are the other foundational Transactions works. All report "significant" variance and computing-time reductions relative to crude non-sequential MCS. Exact speed-up numbers were not retrievable from the abstracts available here.

### Cited Findings
- **González-Fernández, Leite da Silva, Resende, Schilling, "Composite Systems Reliability Evaluation Based on Monte Carlo Simulation and Cross-Entropy Methods," IEEE TPWRS vol. 28, no. 4, pp. 4598-4606, 2013, DOI 10.1109/TPWRS.2013.2267154.** The method combines a CE optimization stage with non-sequential MCS to obtain an auxiliary (IS) distribution of component unavailabilities that minimizes the variance of LOLP/EENS estimators. It was tested on IEEE RTS-79, RTS-96, and a Brazilian system configuration — [ISC record](https://manuscript.isc.ac/Inventory/49/8502.htm); [summary via search of the TPWRS record](https://search.isc.ac/inventory/49/73381.htm)
- **Leite da Silva / Carvalho et al., "Simplified Cross-Entropy Based Approach for Generating Capacity Reliability Assessment," IEEE TPWRS vol. 28, no. 2, pp. 1609-1616, 2013.** This covers generation only (no transmission) and uses an analytical simplification of the CE stage — [INESC TEC repository](https://repositorio.inesctec.pt/items/5cd14649-f05a-43dc-b1bf-ef0cff559e7c/full); [Univ. Lusófona record](https://research.ulusofona.pt/en/publications/simplified-cross-entropy-based-approach-for-generating-capacity-r/)
- **Hua, Bie, Au, Li, Wang, "Extracting Rare Failure Events in Composite System Reliability Evaluation Via Subset Simulation," IEEE TPWRS vol. 30, no. 2, pp. 753-762, 2015, DOI 10.1109/TPWRS.2014.2327753.** Adaptive intermediate failure events are defined through the LP (min load-curtailment) adequacy metric, and conditional samples are generated by MCMC (modified Metropolis). The authors report it is "significantly more efficient than standard Monte Carlo simulation, especially for simulating rare failure events" — [XJTU scholar record](https://scholar.xjtu.edu.cn/zh/publications/extracting-rare-failure-events-in-composite-system-reliability-ev/); [NTU record](https://nanyangtechnologicaluniv.demo.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/extracting-rare-failure-events-in-composite-system-reliability-ev)
- **"Improved Importance Sampling for Reliability Evaluation of Composite Power Systems" (IEEE Xplore doc. 7581049, ca. 2016-2017).** This CE-IS variant also samples load states, not only component states, and reports large efficiency gains over crude MCS. Venue and authors are **[unverified]**; I believe it is IEEE TPWRS, Zhao/ Hou/ ... family, but did not confirm — [IEEE Xplore 7581049](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7581049/)
- **"Enhanced Cross Entropy Method for Composite Power System Reliability Evaluation" (IEEE Xplore doc. 8633934).** It extends CE-IS to correlated continuous variables (wind, load) by fitting a Gaussian mixture model to their joint PDF and doing IS on the modeled density. The listing is IEEE; venue/year **[unverified]**, likely IEEE TPWRS 2019 by Y. Zhao, Y. Tang, W. Li, J. Yu — [IEEE Xplore 8633934](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8633934/)
- **Kim, Bucklew, Dobson, "Splitting Method for Speedy Simulation of Cascading Blackouts," IEEE TPWRS vol. 28, no. 4, 2013.** This is cited as the reference splitting work for cascading failure models. Volume/issue are **[unverified]**; its existence is corroborated by citations in later papers — [arXiv 1711.04491 (citing Kim et al. 2013 for splitting)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.04491)
- **Shortle, Rebennack, Glover, "Transmission-Capacity Expansion for Minimizing Blackout Probabilities," IEEE TPWRS, 2014.** It chooses line additions/upgrades to minimize the probability of a large cascading blackout. Blackout probability comes from MCS with a variance-reduction (splitting / rare-event simulation) technique "because the events of interest are rare" — [ISC record](https://manuscript.isc.ac/Inventory/49/7731.htm)
- **Pre-Transactions / adjacent seminal work.** Belmudes, Ernst, Wehenkel (PMAPS 2008) proposed a general CE framework to find dangerous events, illustrated on IEEE 30-bus static voltage stability — [PMAPS08 PDF](https://people.montefiore.uliege.be/belmudes/Belmudes_PMAPS08.pdf). Wadman, Crommelin, Frank (WSC 2013) applied splitting to grid reliability (curtailment probability), with much lower cost than crude MC at fixed relative error. They warned that a poor choice of time step or importance function can destroy the gains — [CWI](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/22082); [PDF](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~frank011/Articles/WaCrFr13.pdf). A follow-up with Zwart built a large-deviations-based importance function for line-overload splitting. Crude MC needed "tens to millions" of times more samples on examples with up to 11 stochastic injections, and naive distance-based splitting could be slower than crude MC — [CWI 23499](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/23499); [CWI 23702](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/23702)

### Inferences
- The CE-IS template is now standard: optimize the parameters of the component-failure Bernoulli/Markov distributions and load-level distribution with a multilevel CE, then do one IS run. Most later work modifies it in two ways: (a) how continuous/correlated variables are parameterized (GMMs), and (b) how the rare-event threshold is staged (multilevel CE, subset simulation).
- Typical speed-ups (from the broader literature, not confirmed here numerically) are one to two orders of magnitude in CPU time for RTS-79/96-class systems. The writer should treat any specific "x-fold" figure as needing confirmation from the full text.

### Gaps
- Exact speed-up factors (e.g., computational-efficiency ratios on RTS-79/RTS-96) for the 2013 TPWRS CE papers could not be read because IEEE Xplore was unreachable.
- Singh et al.'s TPWRS works on IS/state-space pruning (e.g., Singh and Mitra; Urgun and Singh) could not be verified in this session. A search for Urgun-Singh-Vittal IS + multilabel classification returned nothing.
- Sequential (chronological) CE-MCS in IEEE Trans. was not confirmed. I found no TPWRS sequential-CE paper via search (see Q2 for IET/thesis versions).

## Q2. Recent (2020-2026) papers on IS, CE-IS, adaptive IS, deep-generative IS, and surrogate-assisted IS for reliability or cascading failure

### Takeaway
Since 2020, most new CE-IS work for composite reliability has appeared in IJEPES, IET GTD, IEEE Access, and Chinese journals rather than TPWRS. The verified IEEE-family items are: an adaptive physics-informed mixture IS (IEEE Control Systems Letters 2021); a hybrid multi-surrogate rare-event risk method (IEEE Trans. Smart Grid 2020); and sequential IS for cascading outages (Guo et al., likely TPWRS 2018). Normalizing-flow IS exists in the general reliability literature (RESS 2024; arXiv 2025-2026) but I found no verified IEEE power-systems Transactions paper using flows for G&T reliability.

### Cited Findings
- **Lukashevich, Maximov, "Power Grid Reliability Estimation via Adaptive Importance Sampling," IEEE Control Systems Letters, vol. 6, 2021 (published 2021/2022).** It estimates the probability of line-flow limit violation under a known distribution of renewable injections, using a physics-informed **mixture** proposal with one component per constraint/hyperplane. Mixture weights are adapted by convex optimization, and samples concentrate near the reliability boundary. It reports needing only "a few dozen" samples where plain MC needs thousands, on IEEE test cases — [OSTI record](https://www.osti.gov/pages/biblio/1972176-power-grid-reliability-estimation-via-adaptive-importance-sampling); [arXiv 2105.08753](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.08753)
- **Owen, Maximov, Chertkov, "Importance sampling the union of rare events with an application to power systems analysis," Electronic Journal of Statistics, 2019 (not IEEE).** ALOE mixture IS for the union of J half-space failures (phase-angle-difference limits). It achieves a low coefficient of variation for very small failure probabilities with 10,000 samples. This is foundational for the 2021 mixture-IS work — [arXiv 1710.06965](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.06965); [Project Euclid](https://www.projecteuclid.org/euclid.ejs/1548817590)
- **Xu, Korkali, Mili, Chen, Min, "Risk Assessment of Rare Events in Probabilistic Power Flow via Hybrid Multi-Surrogate Method," IEEE Trans. Smart Grid, vol. 11, no. 2, pp. 1593-, Mar. 2020.** It uses polynomial-chaos surrogates, with a multi-surrogate bank to handle topology uncertainty from random branch outages. The high-fidelity model re-evaluates only tail samples to remove surrogate bias. Tested on three systems — [NSF-PAR](https://par.nsf.gov/biblio/10157224); conference precursor HICSS-53 2020, DOI 10.24251/HICSS.2020.381 — [ScholarSpace](https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/6725ec17-e3d1-4fff-9487-1ebfc6559ed9)
- **Guo, Liu, Wang, Lin, Mei, "Towards High-Efficiency Cascading Outage Simulation and Analysis in Power Systems: A Sequential Importance Sampling Approach," arXiv 1611.00582.** Cascading outages are modeled as a Markov chain, and a SIS strategy is derived "with theoretical justification." It "can significantly bring down the number of simulations and reduce the estimation variance" vs. MC. The journal version is, I believe, IEEE TPWRS vol. 33, no. 3, 2018, retitled "Toward Efficient Cascading Outage Simulation and Probability Analysis in Power Systems" **[venue unverified]** — [arXiv 1611.00582](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.00582). A companion paper, "Quantifying the Influence of Component Failure Probability on Cascading Blackout Risk" (Guo, Liu, Wang, Cao, Mei), uses the same Markov framework, with an IEEE TPWRS ~2018 venue **[unverified]** — [arXiv 1711.02580](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.02580)
- **Composite reliability with CNN + IS (IEEE Xplore doc. 9065985, ca. 2019-2020).** IS is used to generate failure-rich training data, and a CNN replaces the OPF-based state evaluation. It reports much lower computation time on the IEEE RTS. This is a conference paper (likely IJCNN/NAPS), not a Transactions paper **[venue unverified]** — [IEEE Xplore 9065985](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9065985)
- **"Composite System Reliability Analysis using Deep Learning enhanced by Transfer Learning" (IEEE Xplore 9183474, 2020).** CNN plus transfer learning accelerates state classification. Venue (likely PMAPS 2020) is **[unverified]** — [IEEE Xplore 9183474](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9183474/)
- **Manso et al., IET Generation, Transmission & Distribution, vol. 18, no. 2, 2024, DOI 10.1049/gtd2.13000.** It combines quasi-sequential MCS with CE-IS for composite reliability with grid-edge renewables. This is the closest verified "time-dependent CE-MCS" work — [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/8a534a25bd3845ddae779c82dacce7b9). The related PUC-Rio PhD thesis (Manso, 2025, adv. Leite da Silva) covers quasi-sequential MCS, variance reduction (IS/CE), and machine learning for large composite systems — [PUC-Rio thesis](https://www.maxwell.vrac.puc-rio.br/71881/71881.PDF)
- **Zhao et al., IJEPES 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.ijepes.2024.109857.** "An efficient cross-entropy method addressing high-dimensional dependencies for composite systems reliability evaluation" — [Bohrium listing](https://bohrium.dp.tech/paper/arxiv/0609461)
- **Zhao et al., IJEPES vol. 166, 110535, 2025.** "Improved cross-entropy-based power system reliability evaluation using GMM with highly-efficient closed-form parameter updating." GMM IS density for correlated wind speeds and load — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0142061525000869); [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/06880dbc07e14460bcccb4543f5d1edb)
- **Zhao Yuan, Hu Jiaqin, Xie Kaigui, Tang Junjie, "Enhanced Cross-entropy Method With Dependent Importance Sampling for Discrete Variables in Composite Power System Reliability Evaluation," Power System Technology vol. 50, no. 6, pp. 2489-2498, 2026, DOI 10.13335/j.1000-3673.pst.2024.2131.** It replaces independent Bernoulli IS of component states with a discrete mixture model fitted by EM, improving efficiency for high-risk joint-failure loss-of-load events. Tested on RTS-79 and RTS-96 — [PST record](https://epjournal.csee.org.cn/dwjs/en/article/doi/10.13335/j.1000-3673.pst.2023.2036)
- **Xu et al., IEEE Access, 2023.** Well-being (healthy/marginal/at-risk) evaluation with an improved CE method — [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/d6c866b6663946a8af281eabb0d7a6cc)
- **"Three approaches to use Cross-Entropy in reliability evaluation of composite system with high penetration wind energy" (XJTU).** Venue/year not confirmed — [XJTU record](https://scholar.xjtu.edu.cn/en/publications/three-approaches-to-use-cross-entropy-in-reliability-evaluation-o/)
- **Extendable Latin hypercube IS (Sustainable Energy Technologies and Assessments, 2021/2022).** It combines LHS with CE-IS using a truncated Gaussian mixture proposal, applied to composite reliability and wind capacity credit — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2213138821008067); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356631038)
- **Adaptive sequential IS for short-term composite adequacy (Zhejiang Univ.).** CE-based adaptive SIS on a reinforced RBTS, with efficiency gains vs. crude sequential MCS. Venue/year **[unverified]** — [ZJU PDF](https://sgool.zju.edu.cn/publication/paper/Adaptive%20sequential%20importance%20sampling%20technique%20for%20short-term%20composite%20power%20system%20adequacy%20evaluation.pdf)
- **Multilevel Monte Carlo with surrogate models for resource adequacy (arXiv 2203.03437, 2022).** MLMC with surrogate low-fidelity levels. Its reference list points to a CE + subset-simulation composite reliability TPWRS paper (~2021), which I could not independently confirm — [arXiv 2203.03437](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.03437)
- **Normalizing-flow IS (general reliability, not power-specific).** Dasgupta and Johnson, "REIN," Reliability Engineering & System Safety vol. 242, 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.ress.2023.109729: flows learn a map from the input distribution to a quasi-optimal IS density — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/reensy/v242y2024ics0951832023006439.html). Kruse et al. do IS in the latent space of a normalizing flow (arXiv 2501.03394) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.03394v2). There is also "Repulsive normalizing flow mixtures for adaptive importance sampling: reliability analysis of complex systems" (arXiv 2609.21160, 2026) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.21160). Multifidelity GP + normalizing-flow IS (Penn State) — [PSU](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/efficient-reliability-analysis-with-multifidelity-gaussian-proces/)
- **Methodological CE-IS advances that may transfer.** "Safe cross-entropy-based importance sampling" (arXiv 2509.07160) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.07160). Bayesian improved CE with categorical mixtures (arXiv 2309.12490), relevant for discrete component states — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.12490). CE-based adaptive Markov-chain IS with control variates (RESS 2022) — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0951832022006299)
- **Survey.** "Advances in Composite Power System Reliability Assessment: Trends and Machine Learning Role" (2025) discusses IS/CE alongside ML — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391517895)

### Inferences
- For 2020-2026 I found no verified TPWRS/TSG/TSTE paper applying normalizing flows or other deep generative models to construct the IS density for composite G&T reliability. This looks like an open niche, and flow-based IS is mature in the structural-reliability literature (RESS 2024+).
- The dominant recent trend is mixture-based proposals: GMMs for continuous renewables/loads, and discrete mixture models for correlated component outages. ML surrogates are mainly used to replace OPF state evaluation, not to learn the proposal.

Search-engine coverage of IEEE Xplore was poor and Xplore was unreachable, so such papers may exist (e.g., by Zhao/Xie/Tang at Chongqing University, or by Bie's group). ## Q3. Handling high dimensionality (renewables, correlated loads)

### Takeaway
High dimensionality is handled mainly by: (1) Gaussian mixture IS densities for correlated continuous variables, updated in closed form; (2) discrete or dependent mixture models for correlated component states; (3) mixture proposals with one component per failure mode or constraint (ALOE / adaptive mixture IS); (4) stratification or LHS hybrids; and (5) surrogates and multifidelity tail correction. Copula-based dependence appears in adjacent IS work, but I did not verify a copula-IS Transactions paper.

### Cited Findings
- Traditional CE-IS assigns an individual random variable (and CE parameter) to every unit, giving a large space of optimal-distribution parameters. This motivates dimension-reduced or grouped parameterizations — [search summary of CE-IS literature / Zhao et al.](https://bohrium.dp.tech/paper/arxiv/0609461)
- GMM joint density for correlated continuous variables, with IS on the GMM — [Enhanced CE method, IEEE Xplore 8633934](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8633934/). GMM with closed-form parameter updating, IJEPES 2025 — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0142061525000869)
- Discrete mixture model (EM) for dependent IS of component states — [PST 2026](https://epjournal.csee.org.cn/dwjs/en/article/doi/10.13335/j.1000-3673.pst.2023.2036)
- Physics-informed mixture with one component per line-limit hyperplane, with weights from convex optimization — [Lukashevich and Maximov 2021](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.08753); union-of-rare-events ALOE — [Owen et al. 2019](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.06965)
- IS considering multi-state units and wind-load correlation — [IJEPES (S0142061518320520)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0142061518320520)
- LHS + CE-IS with truncated GMM — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2213138821008067)
- Multi-surrogate PCE handles topology-induced non-smoothness, with high-fidelity re-evaluation of tail samples — [Xu et al., TSG 2020](https://par.nsf.gov/biblio/10157224)
- For splitting, a large-deviations rate function as the importance function scales to ~11 stochastic injections, while naive distance-based importance functions fail — [CWI 23702](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/23702)

### Inferences
- The single-Gaussian or independent-Bernoulli CE families degrade with many correlated renewables, because the optimal zero-variance density is multimodal (several distinct failure modes). Mixture families are the practical fix, but they need component-count selection and EM-style updates, and may suffer weight degeneracy in high dimension.

### Gaps
- I found no verified IEEE Trans. paper using copula-based IS proposals or explicit dimension reduction (PCA/latent-space) for the IS density in composite reliability.

## Q4. Cascading failure rare-event methods (splitting, IS, Markov chain)

### Takeaway
The verified core is: splitting (Kim-Bucklew-Dobson TPWRS 2013; Shortle et al. TPWRS 2014, used inside expansion planning); Markov-chain sequential IS (Guo et al., ~TPWRS 2018); and Random Chemistry sampling (Rezaei, Eppstein, Hines). Large-deviation-based splitting (Wadman et al.) covers line overloads. I did not verify a 2020-2026 IEEE Trans. cascading-failure IS/splitting paper in this session.

### Cited Findings
- Standard MC for cascades becomes intractable because blackouts are rare and networks large. IS and splitting can handle fairly complex cascade models but give no structural insight into power-law blackout sizes — [arXiv 1604.03677](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.03677); [arXiv 1711.04491](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.04491)
- Splitting divides the cascade path into sub-paths to raise the probability of the rare event of interest. But each sub-path may still need many samples because the state space is large, which motivated SIS — [Guo et al., arXiv 1611.00582](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.00582)
- Markov-chain SIS reduces the number of simulations and the variance of blackout-risk estimates vs. MC — [arXiv 1611.00582](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1611.00582)
- Random Chemistry sampling (Rezaei, Eppstein, Hines, HICSS 2015) estimated expected large-blackout size about two orders of magnitude faster than MC, with no measurable bias on the 2383-bus Polish case. A TPWRS version exists, I believe ("Estimating Cascading Failure Risk with Random Chemistry," TPWRS 2015) **[unverified]** — [HICSS 2015 PDF](https://phines.w3.uvm.edu/publications/2015/HICSS_RC_2015.pdf)
- Transmission expansion that minimizes cascading-blackout probability uses splitting-type rare-event simulation in the planning loop — [Shortle et al., TPWRS 2014 (ISC)](https://manuscript.isc.ac/Inventory/49/7731.htm)
- Dobson's work on cascading blackout structure and its implications for sampling and mitigation — [Iowa State repository](https://dr.lib.iastate.edu/entities/publication/935de1dc-12d6-49ba-9624-4022127324a8/full)
- A 2026 PES GM paper on sequential IS/cascading appears in the PESGM2026 index (content not verified) — [PESGM2026-000406](https://index.mirasmart.com/PESGM2026/PDFfiles/PESGM2026-000406.pdf)

### Inferences
- Cascading-failure rare-event work peaked around 2013-2018 in TPWRS. Recent effort appears to have moved toward ML screening (e.g., GNN or input-convex NN N-k screening, [arXiv 2410.00796](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.00796)) rather than new unbiased IS/splitting estimators.

### Gaps
- No 2020-2026 IEEE Trans. cascading-failure IS/splitting paper was verified; this needs an IEEE Xplore check.
- No paper was found that uses PMU/synchrophasor or other measurement data to build the IS/splitting proposal for cascading or reliability analysis. Owen et al. use phase-angle-difference failure events (the quantity PMUs measure), but they use model-based, not measured, distributions — [arXiv 1710.06965](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.06965)

## Q5. Transmission expansion planning (TEP) under uncertainty with IS, and known limitations and open problems

### Takeaway
IS/rare-event methods appear in TEP mostly as an inner reliability or blackout-risk evaluator (Shortle et al. 2014; Leite da Silva-school composite reliability used in planning). I found no verified 2020-2026 IEEE Trans. TEP paper whose core contribution is IS. The main open problems are: proposal design for multimodal, high-dimensional, correlated failure domains; time-dependent (sequential) CE; robustness of the CE update ("safe" CE); surrogate bias in tails; and the lack of data-driven or measurement-driven proposals.

### Cited Findings
- Shortle, Rebennack, Glover TPWRS 2014: rare-event simulation (splitting) inside capacity-expansion optimization to minimize blackout probability — [ISC](https://manuscript.isc.ac/Inventory/49/7731.htm)
- Penn State project: Monte Carlo- and IS-based Q-learning for multi-stage stochastic TEP, applied to PJM. This is a project page, not a paper — [PSU project](https://pure.psu.edu/en/projects/efficient-and-scalable-methods-for-multi-stage-transmission-expan/)
- Multi-objective TEP with EENS criterion uses MCS + SVM (EPSR 2023, not IEEE, no IS) — [Maynooth PDF](https://mural.maynoothuniversity.ie/id/eprint/20258/1/EN_multi.pdf)
- Load shedding is often a rare event, which "substantially increases the computational cost of the MCS" for large composite systems. This motivates combining IS/CE with ML — [Manso PhD thesis, PUC-Rio 2025](https://www.maxwell.vrac.puc-rio.br/71881/71881.PDF)
- **Limitation:** in splitting, the choice of importance function (and time step) is the key factor; poor choices can remove the gains or make splitting slower than crude MC — [Wadman thesis](https://dare.uva.nl/id/de499db7-d4a4-4c37-a9fc-410a7779a39a); [CWI 23702](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/23702)
- **Limitation:** single surrogates are biased in low-probability tails and cannot handle topology changes from branch outages. High-fidelity tail correction is needed — [Xu et al., TSG 2020](https://par.nsf.gov/biblio/10157224)
- **Limitation:** independent-Bernoulli CE-IS misses joint/common-mode failure structure — [PST 2026](https://epjournal.csee.org.cn/dwjs/en/article/doi/10.13335/j.1000-3673.pst.2023.2036)
- **Open direction:** "safe" CE-IS to avoid degenerate or overconfident proposals — [arXiv 2509.07160](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.07160). Flow-mixture adaptive IS for multimodal failure domains — [arXiv 2609.21160](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.21160)

### Inferences
- Gaps that a PMU-data IS project could fill:
  - **Measurement-informed proposals.** Learn the IS density, or its initialization, from historical PMU/SCADA operating states (e.g., a flow or GMM fitted to measured angle differences and loadings). No verified prior work exists.
  - **Flow-based IS for G&T reliability and cascading risk in IEEE Trans.** None verified.
  - **Embedding CE-IS reliability evaluation inside TEP optimization**, with proposal reuse across candidate plans.
- When citing speed-ups, distinguish variance-reduction ratio from CPU-time ratio. CE pre-runs and OPF calls dominate cost, so the two can differ widely.

### Gaps
- No PMU/synchrophasor-informed IS paper for reliability, cascading, or planning was found in any venue in this session. A targeted search returned only model-based phase-angle work.
- Full texts, DOIs, and speed-up tables for several IEEE Xplore items (7581049, 8633934, 9065985, 9183474) and the Kim-Bucklew-Dobson and Guo et al. journal versions need direct Xplore verification.
